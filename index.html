Who We Are

A holding company
built from lived
creative work

1218 Arts Holdings LLC is the parent organization behind a growing portfolio of arts, education, and events ventures. Our name marks the day our founding partnership began — and everything we build reflects that origin.

We produce festivals and live experiences, run an arts education academy, and platform emerging artists and performers. We've operated real events, managed real production budgets, and built real communities. The tools we recommend are the ones we've actually used.

This resource hub exists because our audiences trust us — and because we believe in being transparent about the partnerships that sustain our work.
